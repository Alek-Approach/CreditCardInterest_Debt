Monthly payments

  • APR 18%
  • Payment $50/month

b) What will the total cost of the laptop be after 30 months?

Impact of extra payments

You have a $10,000 balance at an APR of 16%.

  • Balance $10,000
  • APR 16%
  • Plan A $300/month
  • Plan B $400/month

a) If you pay $300/month, how much interest will you pay over 44 months?

b) How much could you save in interest by increasing your monthly payment to $400/month?

Show your work
4

Credit card debt vs. savings

You want to buy a $2,000 TV.

  • Price $2,000
  • Card APR 20%, $40/month
  • Savings 3% APY, $200/month

a) If you use a credit card with a 20% APR and only make minimum payments of $40/month, how long will it take to pay it off, and how much will the TV cost in total?

b) If you instead save $200/month in a high-interest savings account at 3% APY, how long will it take to save $2,000, and how much will you earn in interest?
